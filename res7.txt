### What I suspect

If you get a **404 from nginx**, rather than a Keycloak response, the request is probably reaching nginx but **not matching the Ingress rule**.

Typical causes are:

```text
DNS
  ↓
node IP
  ↓
NodePort
  ↓
nginx ingress
  ↓
Host header doesn't match
  ↓
404
```

or:

```text
nginx ingress
  ↓
Ingress class / rule mismatch
  ↓
404
```

Since you just changed the firewall to allow NodePort, there's a good chance the request path has now moved one step further and we're seeing the next issue.
